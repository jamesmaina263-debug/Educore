# Safeguarding procedure: AI study helper (for the named safeguarding contact)

**DRAFT. This adds to, and never replaces, the school's own child protection policy.** The school adapts it and the contact signs below before the feature is switched on.

## What the system does
When a child's question looks like it is about self-harm, abuse, bullying or violence, the system does NOT answer it. It shows the child a kind fixed message telling them to speak to a teacher or trusted adult today, and it saves the message for staff on **Academics, Learning, AI tutor, Learner questions**, under "Worrying messages to follow up". It does not send an email, SMS or WhatsApp message: **nobody is notified automatically.**

## Your duties
1. **Check the screen every school day**, before lessons and at the end of the day. Also check after any day you were away (the deputy covers).
2. For each message: read it; find the child; **speak to the child the same day** in line with the school's child protection policy; do not promise secrecy; do not question the child beyond what the policy allows.
3. Escalate immediately per the policy for any risk to life or any disclosure of abuse (head teacher, then the designated external agencies the policy names, for example the children's officer or police). Do not wait to "mark as followed up".
4. Then press **Mark as followed up** and record the action in the school's own safeguarding log (EduCore keeps no case notes; the question itself is deleted after the retention period).
5. The filter is not perfect. Also look at "Recent questions" for worrying content that was not flagged, and tell EduCore support so the filter can be improved.

## Things not to do
Do not copy a child's question into email or chat. Do not discuss a flagged message with other children. Do not delete the question to hide it.

## Wrong flags
Many flagged messages will be false alarms (for example a story about a bully in a set text). Still mark them as followed up once checked.
